# Share Your Website - Super Simple! 🚀

## Just Run This One Command:

```bash
./scripts/start-public-link.sh
```

Then **copy the link** it shows you and share it with your team!

---

## Even Easier: Use Netlify (Permanent Link)

1. Go to: **https://app.netlify.com/drop**
2. **Drag** your `payment-site` folder onto the page
3. Wait 30 seconds
4. **Copy the link** (like: `https://your-site.netlify.app`)
5. **Share that link** - it works forever!

**No installation needed!** Just drag and drop.

---

## Quick Comparison:

| Method | Time | Permanent? | Easiest? |
|--------|------|------------|----------|
| **Netlify** | 2 min | ✅ Yes | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **`scripts/start-public-link.sh`** | 1 min | ❌ No (stops when you close) | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

**Recommendation:** Use Netlify for a permanent link your team can access anytime!
